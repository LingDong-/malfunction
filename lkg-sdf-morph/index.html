morphing SDF on lenticular display

Drag this window to a Looking Glass Portrait and click on graphics below to fullscreen -- you'll see a donut from one angle and a coffee mug from another -- a visualization of the topologist's joke.

This demo uses a custom script to directly turn a quilted image to a lenticular image in real time, without assuming that you're rendering a 3D scene, (or requiring additional software such as the Looking Glass Bridge), unleashing the device's potential beyond a 3D model/looping video viewer. You can find the script and its documentation here: lkg-lace.js

If you don't own a Looking Glass display, scroll to the bottom of the page for the quilted view that is less utterly confusing to look at on a regular display.
